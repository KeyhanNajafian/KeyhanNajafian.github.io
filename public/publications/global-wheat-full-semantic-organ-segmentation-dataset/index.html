The Global Wheat Full Semantic Organ Segmentation (GWFSS) Dataset

Abstract

Computer vision is increasingly used in farmers' fields and agricultural experiments to quantify important traits. Imaging setups with a sub-millimeter ground sampling distance enable the detection and tracking of plant features, including size, shape, and colour. Although today's AI-driven foundation models segment almost any object in an image, they still fail for complex plant canopies. To improve model performance, the global wheat dataset consortium assembled a diverse set of images from experiments around the globe. After the head detection dataset (GWHD), the new dataset targets a full semantic segmentation (GWFSS) of organs (leaves, stems and spikes) covering all developmental stages. Images were collected by 11 institutions using a wide range of imaging setups.

Key contributions

  • Extends the Global Wheat Dataset Consortium benchmark from head detection to full organ-level semantic segmentation.
  • Covers leaves, stems, and spikes across all wheat developmental stages.
  • Aggregates imagery from 11 institutions with heterogeneous imaging setups, making domain diversity part of the benchmark.
  • Ships a labelled subset of 1,096 ground-truth images alongside a larger unlabelled set, released through the ETH Research Collection.

Methods and techniques

  • Multi-institutional dataset construction
  • Wheat organ semantic segmentation benchmark
  • High-throughput field phenotyping
  • Domain diversity analysis with UMAP

Data and materials

  • GWFSS_v1.0_full plus GWFSS_v1.0_labelled with 1,096 ground-truth labelled images
  • Images from 11 institutions across multiple countries and imaging setups
  • Hosted in the ETH Research Collection (doi:10.3929/ethz-b-000734546)

Applications

  • Benchmarking segmentation foundation models on complex plant canopies
  • Trait extraction for wheat breeding and crop management
  • Cross-domain generalization studies in field phenomics

Related work in Precision Agriculture

A Semi-Self-Supervised Learning Approach for Wheat Head Detection Using Extremely Small Number of Labeled Samples

ICCVW 2021

Trains a wheat head detector from three contoured images and a few minutes of handheld video. The annotated wheat heads are cut out, augmented, and pasted onto background field footage to simulate a large computationally annotated dataset, and two domain adaptation stages then close the gap to real imagery.

Semi-Self-Supervised Learning for Semantic Segmentation in Images with Dense Patterns

Plant Phenomics 2023

Extends the synthesis-plus-domain-adaptation idea from detection to pixel-level segmentation. Two annotated images and one short video clip are enough to train a customized U-Net that reaches a Dice score of 0.89 internally and generalizes to wheat fields from 18 domains across five countries.

Detection of Fusarium Damaged Kernels in Wheat Using Deep Semi-Supervised Learning on a Novel WheatSeedBelt Dataset

ICCVW 2023

Introduces WheatSeedBelt, a 40,420-image single-kernel dataset covering 268 wheat varieties with damage annotations, and uses it to train semi-supervised classifiers for Fusarium damaged kernel grading. An inter-rater study shows human experts do not outperform the model.
