Precision Agriculture Research

Deep learning for crop phenotyping is limited less by model capacity than by annotation cost. Field images of cereal canopies contain hundreds of small, densely packed, self-occluded objects per frame, and pixel-accurate labels for those objects are slow and expensive to produce. Most of my precision agriculture research asks the same question in different forms: how far can a high-performing vision model be pushed when only a handful of human-annotated images exist?

The answer developed across these papers is a family of semi-self-supervised pipelines. A small number of manually annotated images is used to synthesize a large computationally annotated dataset, either by cut-and-paste composition, by generative models such as CycleGAN and denoising diffusion, or by procedural plant models built with L-systems and rendered in a game engine. A model trained on that synthetic data is then moved toward the real data distribution through staged domain adaptation, strong augmentation, and pseudo-labeling with student-teacher refinement.

The tasks span object detection, semantic segmentation, instance segmentation, and video object segmentation, and the crops and sensing setups vary from handheld video to drone flights to controlled seed-belt imaging. Alongside the methods, this work contributes public benchmark data: the WheatSeedBelt single-kernel damage dataset, and, through the Global Wheat Dataset Consortium, the GWFSS wheat organ segmentation dataset.

Publications

A Semi-Self-Supervised Approach for Dense-Pattern Video Object Segmentation

CVPRW 2025 · Keyhan Najafian, Farhad Maleki, Lingling Jin et al.

Extends dense-pattern segmentation from still images to video. Synthetic clips that mimic real camera and object motion are used for pretraining, then pseudo-labeled real video refines the model, which holds up on drone footage across growth stages from boot stage to harvest.

From Semantic To Instance: A Semi-Self-Supervised Learning Approach

Preprint 2025 · Keyhan Najafian, Farhad Maleki, Lingling Jin et al.

Introduces GLMask, an image-mask representation that pushes the model toward shape, texture, and pattern rather than color, and a pipeline that converts semantic segmentation into instance segmentation. Reaches mAP@50 of 98.5% on wheat head instance segmentation and improves COCO by over 12.6 mAP@50.

The Global Wheat Full Semantic Organ Segmentation (GWFSS) Dataset

Plant Phenomics 2025 · Zijian Wang, Radek Zenkl, Latifa Greche et al.

The Global Wheat Dataset Consortium's successor to GWHD. Where GWHD provided bounding boxes for wheat heads, GWFSS provides full semantic segmentation of leaves, stems, and spikes across all developmental stages, assembled from 11 institutions and many different imaging setups.

Beyond Annotations: Efficient Wheat Head Segmentation Using L-Systems, Game Engines, and Student-Teacher Models

ECCVW 2024 · Hosein Beheshtifard, Elijah Mickelson, Keyhan Najafian et al.

Builds the training set from a plant growth model instead of from photographs. Lindenmayer systems describe wheat morphology, a game engine renders the scenes with perfect masks, and a student-teacher loop adapts the resulting model to real fields, reaching a Dice score of 0.87 with no manual annotation.

Efficient Wheat Head Segmentation with Minimal Annotation: A Generative Approach

J. Imaging 2024 · Jaden Myers, Keyhan Najafian, Farhad Maleki et al.

Closes the simulation-to-reality gap with a modified CycleGAN. A large computationally simulated annotated dataset is translated into realistic field imagery, and the resulting model is refined through pseudo-labeling without additional manual annotation.

SynthSet: Generative Diffusion Model for Semantic Segmentation in Precision Agriculture

ECCVW 2024 · Andrew Heschl, Mauricio Murillo, Keyhan Najafian et al.

Generates image and mask pairs from noise. A dual diffusion architecture synthesizes agricultural imagery together with its own pixel-level annotation, with no human labeling at any stage, and super-resolution sharpens the phenotypic detail and image-mask coherence.

Detection of Fusarium Damaged Kernels in Wheat Using Deep Semi-Supervised Learning on a Novel WheatSeedBelt Dataset

ICCVW 2023 · Keyhan Najafian, Lingling Jin, H. Randy Kutcher et al.

Introduces WheatSeedBelt, a 40,420-image single-kernel dataset covering 268 wheat varieties with damage annotations, and uses it to train semi-supervised classifiers for Fusarium damaged kernel grading. An inter-rater study shows human experts do not outperform the model.

Semi-Self-Supervised Learning for Semantic Segmentation in Images with Dense Patterns

Plant Phenomics 2023 · Keyhan Najafian, Alireza Ghanbari, Mahdi Sabet Kish et al.

Extends the synthesis-plus-domain-adaptation idea from detection to pixel-level segmentation. Two annotated images and one short video clip are enough to train a customized U-Net that reaches a Dice score of 0.89 internally and generalizes to wheat fields from 18 domains across five countries.

A Semi-Self-Supervised Learning Approach for Wheat Head Detection Using Extremely Small Number of Labeled Samples

ICCVW 2021 · Keyhan Najafian, Alireza Ghanbari, Ian Stavness et al.

Trains a wheat head detector from three contoured images and a few minutes of handheld video. The annotated wheat heads are cut out, augmented, and pasted onto background field footage to simulate a large computationally annotated dataset, and two domain adaptation stages then close the gap to real imagery.
