GWFSS: Global Wheat Full Semantic Organ Segmentation dataset

A multi-institutional benchmark for full semantic segmentation of wheat organs (leaves, stems, spikes) across all developmental stages, assembled by the Global Wheat Dataset Consortium from 11 institutions using a wide range of imaging setups. Version 1.0 includes 1,096 ground-truth labelled images alongside a larger unlabelled collection, distributed via the ETH Research Collection.

The Global Wheat Full Semantic Organ Segmentation (GWFSS) Dataset

Plant Phenomics 2025

The Global Wheat Dataset Consortium's successor to GWHD. Where GWHD provided bounding boxes for wheat heads, GWFSS provides full semantic segmentation of leaves, stems, and spikes across all developmental stages, assembled from 11 institutions and many different imaging setups.
